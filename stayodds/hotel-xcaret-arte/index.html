Why guests love it

Forbes Four-Star adults-only (16+) 'All-Fun Inclusive' masterpiece featuring turquoise river inlets, dramatic rooftop pools, authentic regional Mexican dining by celebrity chefs, artisan workshops, and unlimited included access to all Xcaret eco-parks and airport shuttles

Know before you book

Natural ocean beach is small and rocky (swimming focuses on the calm turquoise coves and pools); high nightly rates make the most sense if you plan to enjoy Xcaret parks and top-tier Mexican dining
